Privacy Policy

Inchward · Last updated: November 9, 2026

Inchward is a personal focus assistant app. This policy explains what happens to information in and around the app. The short version: your data lives on your phone, and the AI features use your own provider account.

What we collect

Nothing. The app contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking of any kind. We do not operate a server and we cannot see your goals, chats, or activity.

What stays on your device

Everything you create — goals, chats, compressed context, wins, check-ins, settings, and your AI provider API keys — is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. API keys are additionally encrypted using the Android Keystore.

What leaves your device, and to whom (you choose)

  • AI features (onboarding, goal chats, step design, reviews) send the relevant text to the LLM provider you configure (for example Ollama Cloud, Z.AI, OpenAI, Groq, or a custom endpoint), using your own API key. Those requests are governed by your provider's terms and privacy policy.
  • If you enable web search, your search queries are sent to the search provider you configure (Tavily or Brave), using your own API key.
  • If you attach images in chats, those images are sent to your configured AI provider so its vision model can see them.
  • Backups: the export file you create is written only to the location you pick. It contains your goals, chats, wins and settings, but never your API keys.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app removes all local data, including stored (encrypted) API keys. Export files you created are yours to keep or delete wherever you saved them.

Permissions

  • Notifications: used only for the daily check-in reminder you configure. No marketing.
  • Speech recognition (voice input): audio is processed by your device's speech service only when you tap the microphone button; nothing is recorded or stored by us.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page if the app's data practices change, with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email contact@inchward.app.
